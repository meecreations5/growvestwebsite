# GrowVest Motion Update v6

## Header identity

- The full GrowVest wordmark is visible at the top of every page.
- After the visitor scrolls beyond 24 pixels, the wordmark smoothly collapses into the GrowVest icon.
- Navigation and the Investor Portal remain available while the compact icon preserves brand recognition.

## Continuous icon motion

- Flow, bridge and arrow paths move through GrowVest cyan, blue, gold and white.
- The arrow has a restrained upward breathing motion to communicate progress.
- The same animation language is used in the header, homepage hero, closing CTA and loading state.
- All continuous motion is disabled when the visitor has enabled reduced motion.

## Button mouse interaction

- Pill-shaped links and buttons receive a soft pointer-follow highlight.
- Desktop pointer movement creates a maximum 2.8 pixel magnetic shift.
- Pressing a button adds a brief tactile scale response.
- The interaction is automatically disabled on touch-first devices and for reduced-motion users.
