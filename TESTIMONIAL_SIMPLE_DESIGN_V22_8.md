# GrowVest v22.8 — Simple Investor Experience Design

## Objective

Bring the Investor Experiences page and testimonial previews back into the same visual system used by the rest of the GrowVest website.

## Design changes

- Replaced the oversized split-panel hero with the standard GrowVest editorial hero treatment.
- Removed decorative card numbering, alternating dark cards, strong gradients and oversized ornamental shapes.
- Introduced simple white testimonial cards with light borders, subtle shadows and consistent rounded corners.
- Simplified the featured testimonial into one clean card with quote, investor identity and consent status.
- Kept journey filters consistent with the Insights category chips.
- Removed the extra principles-card section to reduce page length and visual noise.
- Simplified Homepage, Insights and About testimonial previews to one clean testimonial card and one supporting content column.
- Preserved consent, disclosure and no-outcome-promise safeguards.
